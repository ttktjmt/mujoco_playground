Trossen WXAI
============

6-DoF + gripper tabletop manipulation arm, in simulation only (``robots/trossen_wxai/sim``): the bimanual model from
`MuJoCo Menagerie <https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie>`_, on a plane or a table. Used by
:doc:`Manipulation <../tasks/rl/manipulation>` (``TrossenPickCube`` and ``TrossenPickCubeVision``, right arm).
